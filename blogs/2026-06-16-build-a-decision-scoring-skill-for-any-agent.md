A tutorial on building an agent skill that structures technical decision-making through a five-step pipeline. The /decide skill guides users through discovery questions, solution research grounded in official documentation, assessment criteria definition, risk matrix evaluation, and mechanical weighted scoring across organizational pillars, with output saved as Architectural Decision Records in markdown.
